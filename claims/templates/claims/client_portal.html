Critical Client Rules for Medical Treatment:
  • Attending to medical appointments: Follow all physician guidance and attend all physical therapy, chiropractic, specialist, and MRI sessions.

Stage 3: Demand Package Preparation & Submission
{% if case.status == 'DEMAND' %}● In Progress{% elif case.status in 'NEGOTIATIONS,NEGOTIATION,LITIGATION,SETTLED' %}✓ Completed{% else %}Upcoming{% endif %}
What happens during this stage:

Our office audits all treatment ledgers and drafts a formal legal demand letter evaluating liability, total medical expenditures, lost wages, and pain and suffering.

Timeframe & Response Window:

The insurance company is given a statutory 30-day timeline to evaluate the demand and respond with an opening offer.

Stage 4: Settlement Negotiations
{% if case.status in 'NEGOTIATIONS,NEGOTIATION' %}● In Progress{% elif case.status in 'LITIGATION,SETTLED' %}✓ Completed{% else %}Upcoming{% endif %}
How negotiations work:

Your attorney engages in structured back-and-forth counter-offers with the insurance adjuster. While insurers attempt to minimize payout, your attorney leverages medical records, treatment history, and damages to advocate for maximum recovery.

Client Authority & Next Steps:

Negotiations require time and patience. No settlement is accepted without your final approval. Once an agreement is reached, we proceed directly to settlement disbursement and medical lien reductions.

Stage 5: Litigation (Court Filing)
{% if case.status == 'LITIGATION' %}● In Progress{% elif case.status == 'SETTLED' %}✓ Completed{% else %}Upcoming{% endif %}
When litigation is required:

If the carrier disputes liability or fails to tender fair value during negotiations, a formal complaint is filed in court, triggering formal discovery and deposition hearings.

What to expect:

While litigation lengthens the process, the vast majority of litigated personal injury files resolve in mediation before ever going to trial.

Stage 6: Settlement Finalization & Disbursement
{% if case.status == 'SETTLED' %}✓ Completed{% else %}Upcoming{% endif %}
Closing & Lien Negotiations:

Once a settlement is agreed upon, the insurer issues settlement funds to the firm trust account. Your attorney then negotiates with medical providers to lower outstanding liens and balances, maximizing your net recovery.

Final Disbursement:

You review and approve the final itemized Settlement Statement. Once approved, proceeds are disbursed with your net balance check issued immediately.
